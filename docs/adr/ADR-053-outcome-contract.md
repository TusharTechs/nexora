# ADR-053: Outcome Contract
Status: Accepted.

Context: Without a formal definition of success, NEXORA calls a mission complete
whenever its plan executes. The user's actual intent may require more, less, or
different work than the plan produced.

Decision: Every mission carries an OutcomeContract generated by LLM at mission
start, validated by Pydantic, and consumed by the compiler, critic, and
semantic verifier. Contract generation failures degrade gracefully to a
minimal default contract so the mission never hard-fails at this stage.

The contract is the source of truth for:
- What to plan (compiler)
- Whether the plan covers the work (critic)
- When the mission is genuinely done (verifier)

Consequences: "Mission complete" means "contract satisfied," not "plan executed."
